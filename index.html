Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our clinic and services.

How to book appointments online?

You can book an appointment by navigating to the "Book Appointment" page from the menu. Fill in your details, select your preferred date and time, and submit the form. You will receive an Appointment ID instantly.

What are the consultation charges?

Our standard general consultation fee is ₹500. Specialist consultations may vary. Please contact our reception desk for specific service charges.

Are emergency services available?

Yes, we provide 24/7 emergency care services. Please call our emergency hotline directly at +91 98765 43210 for immediate assistance.
